Frequently Asked Questions

What time does it start?

At 19:00. For more information regarding the timing of the event please check our timetable page.

Do I need to reserve?

Yes, please reserve your spot in advance through our website. Click the link and fill in all required fields. Unfortunately, we do not accept walk-ins on this special event night.

Do I need to dress up?

Nothing is mandatory for our VIP members, but a costume or your sunday best is encouraged. Previous years most people dressed up.

What is the address?

Papenakkerstraat 8 bus 1.01, 3500 Hasselt.

Where can I park?

Please park along the Sint-truidensteenweg, as to not block our neighbours. Unfortunately valet parking is unavailable at this time.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just your enthousiasm! (and space in your belly)

Are drinks and food provided?

Of course! As gold members of The Karloff, you will have access to a variety of mocktails, sodas and appetizers throughout the evening.

What if I have any food allergies/restrictions?

Please mention anything extra while registering on our website. We will take everything into account and make sure that everyone can enjoy the event.

What if I still have any other questions?

If your question is not listed, please contact either of our general managers.
